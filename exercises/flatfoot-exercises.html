Short Foot Exercise (SFE) · Tibialis Posterior Strengthening · Recommended by Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation Specialist Physicians

IMPORTANT GUIDELINES
  • The Short Foot Exercise (SFE / Arch Doming) is the most evidence-based — prioritize it.
  • Wear prescribed orthotics consistently — exercises + orthotics together are significantly more effective.
  • Stop if sharp pain increases — mild arch muscle fatigue is normal and expected.
  • Avoid prolonged walking barefoot on hard surfaces until arch strength improves.
  • Use supportive footwear with firm heel counter and medial arch support daily.
  • Follow up with your FTR specialist every 3–6 months.
  • These exercises are general — not a substitute for individual physiotherapy assessment.
  • Monitor body weight — excess weight significantly increases arch loading.
