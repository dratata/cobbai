IMPORTANT GUIDELINES
  • Perform exercises regularly and with correct form — consistency matters more than intensity.
  • Do not hold your breath — coordinate breathing with each movement (Schroth RAB breathing).
  • Stop immediately if any exercise causes increased pain, numbness, or breathing difficulty.
  • These exercises are general — for a curve-specific Schroth program, see a certified BSPTS/Schroth physiotherapist.
  • If wearing a TLSO brace, ask your physiotherapist which exercises to perform inside vs. outside the brace.
  • Re-evaluate with your FTR specialist every 3–4 months.
  • Stand tall during daily life — avoid prolonged sitting or slouching.
  • Ensure adequate calcium and Vitamin D intake for bone health.
